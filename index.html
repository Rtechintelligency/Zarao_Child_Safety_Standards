Child Safety Standards

Zarao is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation on our platform.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Zero-tolerance policy

Zarao strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), and any behaviour that endangers or sexually exploits a child. This policy applies to every user and all content and interactions on Zarao.

1. Prohibited content and behaviour

Users must not create, upload, share, request, store, promote, or distribute CSAM or any content that depicts, encourages, facilitates, or normalizes the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child.

The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Child sexual abuse material, including images, videos, or other visual content involving the sexual exploitation of a minor.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or attempts to establish contact with a child for sexual purposes.
  • Sexual exploitation, trafficking, coercion, blackmail, or threats involving a child.
  • Sharing, requesting, or threatening to share sexual content involving a minor.
  • Any attempt to use Zarao to identify, target, exploit, or endanger children.

Attempts to evade these rules, including by using private messages, temporary content, or another account, are also prohibited.

2. Reporting concerns

Users should promptly report content, accounts, messages, or behaviour that may involve child safety concerns. Reports should include enough information for Zarao's safety team to identify and review the concern. Do not download, copy, or redistribute suspected illegal material in order to report it.

  • In Zarao: Use the in-app report or feedback feature, where available, to report the relevant account, content, or interaction.
  • By email: Contact our designated child safety point of contact at the address listed in the contact section below.

If a child may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law enforcement.

3. Review and enforcement

Zarao reviews child-safety reports and takes appropriate action in accordance with this policy and applicable law. Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

  • Removing prohibited content, including CSAM, once identified.
  • Restricting, suspending, or permanently terminating accounts involved in violations.
  • Preserving relevant information where legally permitted or required.
  • Escalating serious or confirmed cases to the appropriate competent authorities, including the relevant regional authority or the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), where applicable and legally appropriate.

We do not permit users to use Zarao to facilitate child abuse or exploitation. We may take action while a report is being assessed when necessary to protect users.

4. Compliance with child safety laws

Zarao is committed to complying with applicable child protection and child safety laws and regulations in the jurisdictions where the service operates. We will assess reports of suspected CSAM and make reports to the appropriate authority when required by applicable law.

5. Scope and updates

These standards apply to Zarao and all people who use the service. Zarao may update this page as its safety practices or applicable requirements change. The current version will remain publicly accessible at this URL.
